## Evidence Promotion Rule

Move a row to `Proven` only after recording the exact command, environment,
sanitized response, and log or metric evidence in `results.md` or a
linked runbook. If evidence does not exist, write `Not implemented` or `Blocked`
with the exact reason; do not leave vague status text.
